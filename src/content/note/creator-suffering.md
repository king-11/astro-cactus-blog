---
title: Creator Suffering
description: Thoughts about building good projects during a hackathon.
publishDate: "2025-08-21T02:00:00Z"
---

I don't believe that you can build a great product without ever encountering the problem you are trying to solve.

Getting people's opinions and feedback is secondary source of truth, *suffering is primary*.

So rather than going on a hunt for startup idea, I think it's better to just be more **mindful** and **curious** when you encounter the next problem.

A few key points also noted by Paul Graham in his [blog](https://paulgraham.com/startupideas.html) on what are best startup ideas:
- they're something the founders themselves want
- that they themselves can build
- that few others realize are worth doing
